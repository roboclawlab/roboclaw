# RoboClawLab Notes
RoboClawLab is a powerful open-core framework developed by andyRobot that converts your creative
vision into a fully synchronized live robotic production. RoboclawLab intelligently connects robotics with
multiple AI models, precise timing engines, and physical hardware into one cohesive, autonomous
creative production studio.

An AI-system-independent architecture. RoboClawLab connects to any large language model or
generative AI service running locally on your network, on-premise servers, or in the cloud. Multiple models can operate simultaneously,
each contributing according to your strategic needs.

This independence is powered by our closed-loop architecture which continuously monitors live
performance data, timing accuracy, robot feedback, lighting state, camera cuts, audience response,
and more.
